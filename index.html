Welcome to Tonika

A school built around the student, not the syllabus

The tonic is the note every melody comes home to. We named our school after it because that is how learning should feel: a clear direction, a sense of arrival, and the joy of playing music you love.

At Tonika every student follows a personal path – solo performer, band musician, composer or early-years explorer – with lesson plans that adapt to your pace and goals.

FAQ

Questions parents and students ask

How does the free trial lesson work?

You pick an instrument and a convenient time. A teacher spends 30 minutes with you (or your child), checks the level and goals, and suggests a path. No payment details are needed.

Do I need my own instrument?

Not at the beginning. Our classrooms are fully equipped, and we help you choose and rent or buy an instrument when you are ready.

From what age can my child start?

Little Tonika welcomes children from birth to 5 years together with a parent. Individual instrument lessons start at 5+.

Where are the lessons held?

All lessons take place at our school in Wilanów, Warsaw. Selected theory and composition sessions are also available online.

Can adults join?

Absolutely. Around a third of our students are adults – complete beginners and returning players alike.
